## Sources and checks for every figure

**FAA awards (reserve lines).** Source: USASpending.gov award records (`/api/v2/awards/<id>/`, pulled 2026-10-01, 53 FAA awards to the City, `raw/aviation/faa_awards_usaspending.json`). Place of performance zip 60666 is O'Hare and 60638 is Midway. Piece = obligation minus outlay (a missing outlay counts as zero), only awards under ALN 20.106 or 20.116 (the Airport Improvement Program), made in federal fiscal year 2025 or earlier, still open in 2026. Check: every one of these awards also appears with the same grant number, project summary and the same dollar amount in the FAA AIP grant histories (FY2021 to FY2025 workbooks, `raw/aviation/FY*-AIP-grants.xlsx`, https://www.faa.gov/airports/aip/grant_histories). `meta.faa_workbook_mismatches` in the JSON is empty, so zero amount differences. Example: grant 3-17-0022-198-2025 is $30,000,000 in both. Totals by group (USASpending): O'Hare FY2025 and earlier AIP 26 awards, $485,666,300.66 obligated, $385,531,522.21 paid out; Midway 16 awards, $160,277,807.97 obligated, $136,232,399.43 paid out.
 - Why only part of the carryover is named. Summary G (Mayor's Budget Recommendations 2026, p.607, PDF 616) calls the reserve a carryover estimated on Aug 1 2025: $411,790,000 (O'Hare), $123,869,000 (Midway), plus anticipated 2026 grants of $112,740,000 and $36,199,000. A year later most of the older awards are already paid out. The unspent part of FY2025-and-earlier awards ($100.1M and $24.1M) is what public data can name. The remaining $311.7M and $99.8M is not tied to anything public. It is likely partly the FAA letter-of-intent and entitlement money the City expects (the O'Hare OS says $20M of a final LOI grant is paid in federal fiscal year 2026, 2026CD OS Table B-3), and partly budget room, but that is a guess, so the box says only "not tied to a named FAA award".
 - FY2026 awards (October to September 2026, ALN 20.116 and the infrastructure law airport grants 20.117) are not in any FAA grant-history file yet (FAA has no FY2026 page, HTTP 404 on 2026-10-01). USASpending: O'Hare 5 awards, $20,499,999 (20.116) plus $83,314,240 (20.117); Midway 2 awards $5,675,308 (20.116) plus 2 awards $21,410,235 (20.117). The two Midway 20.116 awards sit on 925F-2805-0140, which is the 2026 anticipated money for Midway AIP. The 20.117 awards are not placed in a box: Summary G lists a separate "O'Hare (BIL)" grant 925F-2827 with $90M anticipated and no ordinance row, so the match would be a guess.
 - Rule check: pieces never exceed the line (a build assertion), and all unspent amounts are positive.

**Series 2010B (Build America Bonds).** Outstanding after the 2026CD refunding: $328,000,000 (2026CD OS p.54 table, FS2025 p.50 "interest at 6.145%-6.845%"). Of the 2010B bonds, the $250,000,000 6.845% term bonds due 2038 and $315,500,000 6.395% bonds due 2040 and $12,500,000 6.145% bonds due 2040 were issued (2010 OS cover). The 2020 OS lists the 6.845% maturity as called on 10/26/2020, so the $328M outstanding is the 6.395% ($315.5M) and 6.145% ($12.5M) bonds. Interest = 315,500,000 x 6.395% + 12,500,000 x 6.145% = $20,176,225 + $768,125 = **$20,944,350**. Independent check: 35% BAB subsidy x (1 - 5.7% sequestration) = $6,912,683, and the 2026CD OS Table B-4 prints "BAB Subsidy Applied to Debt Service on Series 2010B (6,912)" thousand for 2026 (p.E-156). The script asserts this within $1,000. No principal falls due in the window (sinking fund starts 2035 for the 2038 bonds and 2038 for the 2040 bonds). Caveat: 2010B is a third lien bond that became senior lien, the interest is gross of the subsidy (the subsidy is a revenue, not a reduction of the ordinance line, which is why the OS says "does not include any adjustment for BAB subsidy").
 - Remaining residual $58,717,912 on the interest line: parts of 2016D to 2016G, 2017A to 2017C and 2018C. For 2018C the OS prints only two $400M term bonds (4.472% and 4.572%), but it was partly defeased by 2025C, so the outstanding balance $710.13M is known but the split between the two coupons is not. A coupon estimate would be a guess, so it was not done. The principal residual ($46,676,911) has no printed per-series principal and was left alone.

**PFC (passenger facility charges).** FAA "PFC approved locations, collections and expiration dates" workbook as of 2026-09-30 (https://www.faa.gov/airports/pfc/monthly_reports, `raw/aviation/pfc_locations_latest.xlsx`): O'Hare $4.50 per passenger from 2001-04-01, total approved $6,560,089,985, expires 2039-02-01. Midway $4.50 from 2007-01-01, $2,477,196,685, expires 2056-05-01. The O'Hare 2026CD OS says authority of $6.55 billion as of March 31, 2026 (p.60), consistent. These totals are attached as side info on the reserve lines. **FAA does not publish project lists with amounts in these files**, and the FAA's "PFC decisions" tables are per application, with no ORD or MDW rows in the 2023 and 2024 files, so PFC money could not be split into projects. PFC applied to O'Hare debt service in 2026: $111,658,000 (2026CD OS Table B-4), attached as side info on the O'Hare interest residual.

**O'Hare capital program (O'Hare 21, ORDNext).** 2026CD OS, Report of the Airport Consultant, Table S-1 and Table 2-1 (pdf pp. E-19, E-43 to E-46), attached as side info on the only O'Hare capital line in the ordinance, `0740-2005-9047` ($2,000,000): total capital program $14,627,114,000 (escalated), of which previously funded $5,537,774,000 (earlier bonds plus about $290.1M of executed grants), 2026B bonds $1,004,658,000, future bonds $7,484,681,000, other funding $600,000,000; by program TAP Phase 1 $9,826,217,000, pre-approved CIP $1,925,810,000, allowances $768,157,000, ongoing earlier approvals $209,218,000, new approvals since 2018 $1,493,760,000, proposed $403,952,000. Table 2-1 (2018 dollars) gives the ten largest TAP elements: Terminal 2/Global Terminal $1,830,000,000, Baggage handling $825,000,000, Concourse D $730,000,000, Consolidated people mover and tunnel $665,000,000, Concourse E $527,000,000, utilities $493,000,000, Terminal 5 expansion $250,000,000, detention basin $223,000,000, Terminal 5 landside $222,000,000, western parking $215,000,000. **These are program costs over about ten years, not 2026 spending, and the airport building program is not appropriated line by line in the ordinance** (it is paid with airport bond proceeds and airline fees under the Airline Use and Lease Agreement), so they are shown beside the line and not inside it. The same plan is why O'Hare "construction" in the ordinance is so small.
 - Midway 2025 OS, 2025-2029 capital improvement program: $660.4M, uses airfield $342.3M, terminal $165.9M, facilities/fuel/vehicles $59.5M, noise $56.1M, parking $18.5M, land $18.0M; sources prior bonds $82.3M, grants $68.6M, 2025 bonds $165.7M, future bonds $343.7M (OS pp.53 to 55).
